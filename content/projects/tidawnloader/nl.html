Another project I'm working on is a self-hosted Tidal music downloader and manager.


Since there isn't really a good way to download and manage music and easily download in high quality, I decided to create tidawnloader.


It's made with C#, Blazor, and a dockerfile which gets build by github workflows for easy distribution.


Tidal is a music streaming service that has music in lossless quality, this means that it is much higher quality than spotify and youtube music.


I found an existing open source project that allows you to listen to tidal's music for free called monochrome

This helped me a lot with finding ways to download music from Tidal for completel free.
